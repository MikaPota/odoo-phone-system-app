BIZZKICKR

Your business card with a QR code, and your business network, inside Odoo. Free.

Show your card on the phone at a trade show. Whoever scans it asks to connect, confirms their address, and once you confirm, they are a contact in Odoo.

How it works

  • An administrator connects the database to GCN-ALLIANCE: a free account, nothing is charged
  • Each user registers with their email address and creates their card from what Odoo knows
  • The card's QR code leads to its BIZZKICKR page, where people ask to connect; nobody is connected until they confirm their address and you confirm them
  • "Send my card" on any contact mails your card with a link to connect
  • Confirmed connections become contacts in Odoo and stay up to date; what you change in Odoo stays as you changed it

Where your data is

Cards, requests and connections are kept by BIZZKICKR in the EU. GCN-ALLIANCE provides BIZZKICKR for Odoo and keeps no card data itself. Each person confirms by email before anything is shared, and can withdraw at any time.
